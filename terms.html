1. About these terms

These terms apply to all work by JD-Style and Design ("we", "us") for you, the client. By sending a request through our quiz, accepting an estimate, or paying an invoice, you agree to them. Your client ID appears on your estimate, invoices and receipts. Please quote it when you contact us.

2. Services

We design and build websites for individuals, businesses, events and organisations. Optional extras include domains, logo design, analytics and social media setup. Ongoing services, such as Advanced SEO, are available to businesses and organisations. What's included in your project is listed on your estimate and invoice.

3. Estimates and quotes

The estimate from our quiz is a guide. Prices may change, and they can be negotiated before work starts. The price we agree with you, shown on your invoices, is the one that applies. Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately.

4. Payments

Website projects are paid in three parts that together make up 100% of the project price:

  1. Commitment fee, 15%: due before work starts. It reserves your place and starts the project.
  2. Half-completion payment, 35%: due when the site is halfway done.
  3. Final payment, 50%: due on completion, before ownership of the site is handed over to you.

Invoices are due within the time shown on them. We accept bank transfer, Visa and Mastercard debit or credit cards, PayPal and Lynk. Card and PayPal payments are handled by PayPal and charged in US dollars at the exchange rate shown when you pay. Your bank or card provider may charge its own fees. We may pause work while a payment is overdue.

5. Recurring services

Recurring services are billed on the cycle you choose: bi-weekly, monthly, every 3 months, every 6 months or yearly. Longer plans may include a discount. Billing starts after your site's first month unless we agree otherwise. An invoice is sent at the start of each cycle. You can cancel at any time before your next billing date; the service then continues until the end of the period you've paid for.

6. Refunds

  • You can ask for a refund within 14 days of making a payment, using the "Request a refund" option on your receipt or by contacting us.
  • The commitment fee is non-refundable once work on your project has started.
  • Payments for work that has been completed and approved, and for third-party costs we have already paid for you (such as domain registration), can't be refunded.
  • The final payment can't be refunded once ownership of the site has been handed over to you.
  • Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method, or by bank transfer, within a reasonable time. We may offer a partial refund for work already done.
  • Requests made after the refund window are at our discretion.

7. Revisions and changes
